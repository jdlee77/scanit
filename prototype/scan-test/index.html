  • Hold the phone about a hand's width (15–25 cm) away and use zoom, rather than pushing the phone right up to the label. Most phone cameras can't focus closer than that.
  • Turn a long barcode so its bars run across the box, side to side.
  • If a label won't read live, tap the picture for a still shot, or use "Take a photo" below.
